Curious Minds

Learning how to think, not just what to remember

Curious Minds helps children discover how to notice, ask, explore and understand — so learning feels like a shared adventure rather than a list of facts to memorise.

  • Children begin by noticing something in the world around them.
  • Questions are welcomed, not hurried away.
  • Imagination comes before correction.
  • Ideas are explored together, through stories, talk and simple discovery.
  • Explanations are truthful and age-appropriate.
  • Uncertainty is acknowledged honestly when the answer is still unfolding.
  • Learning becomes part of everyday family conversation.

How we learn

Three habits that keep curiosity alive

Notice and wonder

Curiosity often begins with something small: a shape in the sky, a sound in the garden or a question at bedtime.

Explore together

Stories encourage children and adults to talk, observe, draw, test ideas and discover answers together.

Understand honestly

Ideas are explained in clear, age-appropriate language without replacing the real science with misleading shortcuts.

Family learning

Curiosity grows when it is shared

Curious Minds books are designed to be read and discussed together.

A story can open a question; a short conversation, a drawing or a simple observation can deepen it. Adults have gentle opportunities to extend a child’s learning without interrupting the adventure on the page.

Looking ahead

More questions are waiting

Future Curious Minds books and resources will explore science, nature, creativity, practical life lessons and curriculum-linked learning across different age ranges.

The aim is always the same: to help children remain curious, thoughtful and confident about asking why.
